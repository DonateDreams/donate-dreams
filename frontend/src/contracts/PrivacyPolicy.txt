This Privacy Policy explains how Donate Dreams (“Donate Dreams,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Service.

1. The Current V3 Architecture

The current version of Donate Dreams is designed primarily as a client-side application.

Certain account and profile information may be stored locally in your web browser using technologies such as localStorage.

This means that information stored locally may remain on your device/browser and may not automatically be available on another device.

The current V3 version does not operate as a centralized database for all user accounts.

This policy should be updated if Donate Dreams later introduces a centralized backend.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, Donate Dreams may allow you to enter:

username;
password;
biography;
personal story;
donation goal;
cryptocurrency wallet address;
profile information; and
other information you voluntarily submit.

Do not submit sensitive personal information unless the Service specifically requests it.

3. Cryptocurrency Wallet Addresses

A public cryptocurrency wallet address may be displayed publicly as part of a user's profile.

Never submit a private key or seed phrase.

A public wallet address should be treated as public blockchain information.

4. Information Stored Locally

Information stored through browser-based storage may remain on your device until:

you delete it;
the application deletes it;
your browser removes it; or
you clear your browser's stored website data.

Because this information is stored locally, clearing browser data can result in loss of access to locally stored account information.

5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

In the current V3 architecture, Donate Dreams is not intentionally designed to collect information such as:

Social Security numbers;
government identification numbers;
credit card numbers;
cryptocurrency private keys;
cryptocurrency seed phrases; or
bank account credentials.

Do not submit these types of information to Donate Dreams.

6. Third-Party Services

Donate Dreams may use third-party services, libraries, APIs, hosting providers, wallet providers, analytics services, or other technologies.

If such services are added, this Privacy Policy should be updated to accurately describe:

what information they receive;
why they receive it;
how long they retain it; and
where applicable, how users can exercise privacy choices.
7. Blockchain Information

Blockchain transactions may create publicly visible records.

Donate Dreams does not control blockchain networks.

If you send cryptocurrency, information associated with that transaction may be publicly available through the relevant blockchain explorer.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the current design of the Service.

However, no website, browser, computer, blockchain, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for securing their own devices, passwords, and cryptocurrency wallets.
