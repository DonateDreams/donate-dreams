⚠️ READ BEFORE SENDING CRYPTOCURRENCY

Donate Dreams facilitates connections between people who wish to provide cryptocurrency support and people who wish to receive it.

Donate Dreams does not guarantee that a recipient deserves a donation, that a recipient's story is accurate, or that donated funds will be used for any particular purpose.

You are responsible for deciding whether to donate.

1. Verify the Recipient

Before sending cryptocurrency:

CHECK THE WALLET ADDRESS.

Make sure:

the address is correct;
the network is correct;
the recipient is the person you intend to support; and
the amount is correct.

A valid wallet address can still belong to the wrong person.

Donate Dreams' address validation can help identify malformed addresses, but it does not prove that a user owns the wallet address they entered.

2. Transactions May Be Irreversible

Cryptocurrency transactions are generally irreversible after confirmation.

Donate Dreams generally cannot:

cancel a blockchain transaction;
reverse a transaction;
retrieve funds;
change the recipient;
recover cryptocurrency sent to the wrong address; or
recover cryptocurrency lost because of wallet compromise.
3. No Guarantee of Recipient Identity

A profile's username, story, biography, photographs, or other information does not necessarily establish the user's real-world identity.

Users should independently evaluate information before sending money.

4. Cryptocurrency Value

Cryptocurrency prices can change rapidly.

The value of a donation may increase or decrease after it is sent.

Donate Dreams does not guarantee any particular value or return.

5. Network Fees

Blockchain transactions may involve network or transaction fees.

These fees are determined by the applicable blockchain, wallet, or transaction mechanism and may change over time.

6. Scams and Fraud

Users should be cautious of:

impersonation;
fabricated stories;
pressure to donate immediately;
requests for private keys;
requests for seed phrases;
fake wallet addresses;
phishing links;
investment promises; and
requests to send cryptocurrency to unrelated addresses.

Donate Dreams will never require you to provide a private key or seed phrase in order to receive a donation.

7. No Investment Opportunity

Donations made through Donate Dreams are not investments.

A donation should not be made with an expectation of:

profit;
repayment;
interest;
ownership;
guaranteed returns; or
financial appreciation.
8. Tax and Legal Considerations

Donate Dreams does not determine whether a particular cryptocurrency donation is:

tax deductible;
a charitable contribution;
taxable income;
a gift;
subject to reporting requirements; or
otherwise subject to a particular legal treatment.

Users are responsible for obtaining their own tax or legal advice where appropriate.

9. Donation Confirmation

Before confirming a donation, users should verify the displayed:

Recipient → Wallet Address → Network → Amount

If any information appears incorrect, do not proceed.
